Managing leave for variable-hour employees—those with schedules and hours that change from week to week—can present real compliance challenges for HR teams. Unlike staff with fixed schedules, variable-hour employees require specific methods for determining eligibility, tracking accrual, and staying compliant with federal, state, and local leave laws. Without clear processes, businesses risk costly penalties, operational issues, and potential legal action.
