# AllWork

> AllWork is a New York-based flexible-workforce management and payments platform. It onboards, schedules, tracks, pays and compliantly classifies contingent labor — W-2 and 1099 — acting as both Employer of Record (EOR) and Agent of Record (AOR) for brands in beauty and luxury, retail, food and beverage, oil and gas, technology, life sciences, financial services and self-direction programs.

AllWork publishes **no public developer program**: there is no API reference, no SDK, no
webhook catalog and no machine-readable specification (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL or
Postman collection) anywhere on its site. `api.allworknow.com`, `developers.allworknow.com`
and `docs.allworknow.com` do not resolve. The only machine-readable contracts AllWork serves
anonymously are its OpenID Connect discovery document and an RFC 9116 `security.txt`.

This file is generated by API Evangelist from publicly reachable material. It is not
